# Buyer Concentration Chart for LPs

## Overview

Liquidity Providers (LPs) require dedicated risk monitoring of their portfolio exposure to individual buyers. The **Buyer Concentration** chart provides a clear breakdown of funded exposure across buyers with exact USDC totals and percentage denominators.

## User Interaction

- **Analytics & LP Views**: Accessible directly in LP-focused dashboard sections.
- **Totals & Denominators**: Displays the aggregate funded exposure alongside individual buyer shares.
- **Missing Identity State**: Surfaces an explicit warning notice when funded invoices lack valid buyer identity data.
